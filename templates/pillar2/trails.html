Trail Making Test A - Protocol
  • Present the Trails A sheet with numbered circles (1-25)
  • Patient connects circles in numerical order (1→2→3→...→25)
  • Start timing when patient begins
  • Stop timing when patient reaches circle 25
  • If error made, immediately point it out and patient corrects (time continues)
Instructions: "On this page are numbers from 1 to 25. Draw a line connecting the numbers in order, starting with 1, then 2, then 3, and so on until you reach 25. Work as quickly as you can. Do not lift your pencil from the paper. Begin."

Trail Making Test B - Protocol

  • Patient alternates between numbers and letters (1→A→2→B→3→C→...→13)
  • Start timing when patient begins
  • Stop timing when patient reaches circle 13
  • If error made, immediately point it out and patient corrects (time continues)
Instructions: "On this page are numbers and letters. Draw a line alternating between numbers and letters in order. Start with 1, then go to A, then 2, then B, and so on until you reach the end. Work as quickly as you can. Begin."
